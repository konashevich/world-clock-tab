## Detailed description

World Clock Tab turns your browser’s New Tab page into a calm, full-screen world clock. Each city appears as a vertical column with a photo backdrop, the city name, local time, zone abbreviation, GMT offset, and date.

**New Tab home page**

Open a new tab and see your cities at a glance. The home page is display-only: no settings buttons, no edit controls—just photos and times.

**Extension panel**

Click the pinned toolbar icon to manage everything: add or remove cities (up to eight), reorder columns with ‹ and › buttons, change photos, open settings, and choose which city drives the toolbar analog clock.

**Toolbar analog clock**

Select any city on the home tab or in the panel. The browser toolbar icon becomes an analog clock for that time zone (no seconds hand on the icon).

**Clock options**

24-hour time is on by default. Seconds are optional and appear on the digital clocks only, not on the toolbar icon.

**City photos**

Photos are fetched from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons when you add a city, then cached locally. You can upload your own image for any city and restore the Wikipedia photo later.

**Defaults**

Sydney, Kyiv, Rome, and London are included on first install. Duplicate time zones (including IANA aliases such as Kyiv/Kiev) are rejected. You cannot delete the last remaining city.

**Privacy**

Your city list, settings, photo cache, and optional photo overrides stay in your browser. World Clock Tab does not use analytics and does not send your data to a first-party server. Wikipedia is contacted only to look up city article summaries and images.
